with low-born people. It does not resonate well with me because I know for a
fact that a person's origin does not define their value.

## Baudolino

OK, here the story starts to get fun. Baudolino found accidentally "holy" relics
of the three Wise Men who visited the newborn Jesus. Now, once they had
transported them to the Emperor's territory, they need an even better miracle on
the Empire's list: the Kingdom of Prester John.

This is about forming a story out of existing small facts: it's building a
mosaic of the greatest Empire. I understand that, I saw it many times in my
reality: political narratives get common threads in which the storytelling
happens.

Even though I have already read "The Prague Cemetery", this motif is still
fascinating.
