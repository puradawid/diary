# 2026-10-27 Tuesday Evening

Thinking of life gives me this: what's the point? What do I want to do? There is
an answer that breaks heart everyone: nothing, I just want to cease to exist.

Life has no meaning. Heck, you can live as hobo, you can live as a millionaire -
it's the same outcome afterwards: you're dead. Why people bother? Because they
have the mechanism in their heads that asks them to live. What if this mechanism
breaks? Well, they kill themselves.

This is what happens to me now: I see no reason and I want simply to kill
myself, whatever is the mean used. Despite the pain, the suffering, the
environment: I want to stop existing. Stop breathing.

Some people treat this as a symptom of weak brain. Tired, exhausted. But they're
wrong. They cannot see the abyss because their brains are hiding the truth from
them. Swept under the carpet, they wait until it bangs them again.

I'm trying to verbalize this as much as possible. In other words: a man lives
the entire life, doing something, and that effect is meaningless. Even so, if
they are living only for their own pleasure, the pleasure is gone and they are
gone.

Lucky those who can see the pleasure in life. I cannot. The only thing that I
see that makes sense is the curiosity: the need to obtain more and more
knowledge about reality. But this is also wearing off, at the end of the day.

In other words, trying to explain myself even more: death is the ultimate
solution to life. And the road that you're taking to it may not have meaning.
Well, for you it may have, but for me: I see no reason to live. I wake up, work,
go to sleep, watching or reading. I am basically dead. I want nothing, I want
just to die. This is my real goal here: die.

What I cannot see here? This is the question I don't understand.

I look from the time to time out of the window, assesing what would be the
outcome if I will jump out of it. I am not panicing like Tolstoy, worrying that
I will kill myself - I am not worried. I am counting on it.
