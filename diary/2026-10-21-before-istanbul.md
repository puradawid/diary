## Voltaire and his philosophical stories

I came back to reading Voltaire's Candide. This story is pretty abstract, and I
find myself struggling to read it. The comedy he is using is way over the
counter and good taste: it's a bit of pornography, violence and absurd mixed
together to expose life's inconsistency, chaos and all of that things we human
beings tend to avoid in life.

Criticism of Christian faith, although well-deserved, is a bit outdated. The old
system has gone, I think it is valuable to speak about it, however not that
important to see it now. This is a bit rusty, however interesting reading.

And I do like when characters are just raising up from the dead. Absurdness of
the story makes it even more surprising, perhaps it is even a comedy.
